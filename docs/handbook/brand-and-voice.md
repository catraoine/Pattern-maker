# Brand and voice

## Name and pitch

- **Maison Tracé** (picked Oct 14, 2026; replaced "Pattern Maker", which clashes with Wild Ginger's
  PatternMaker). Domain **maisontrace.co**. Wordmark MAISON TRACÉ in Chakra Petch caps, never restyled.
- **Pitch:** "The piece you have in mind, drafted to you." Longer: "Snap it, sketch it or style it,
  and Maison Tracé drafts the pattern to your measurements." Positioning: a fashion house in your
  pocket, not a craft tool.
- **Bio line** (social, "for now"): "Snap a look you love. Get the sewing pattern, made to measure."
- Customer line to reuse: "that one piece, but made for me."
- **Audience:** first the new wave (18 to 35, style-led, arrived via phone), then keen hobbyists
  (testers, credibility, Maker tier). Returners welcome, not targeted.

## Words

- **Never** lead with plus-size or "standard sizing leaves people out" framing (Cat is sick of it).
  The promise is creative control: the exact piece you want, made to fit you.
- **AI stance** (Oct 28): "Your pattern is drafted, not generated." AI only reads a photo or a
  description into style choices; drafting, fit checks, drawings and files are code. Be open about
  it on the photo screen, never lead with it. Avoid "AI-generated", "AI-powered", "smart pattern",
  "perfect fit". No AI images, AI models or pretend-expert text.
- Measurement wording is about the tape, never the body (no BMI or weight-judging words).
- Every caption, bio and post is checked against the "Meet the sewist" research and the AI stance
  word list (both linked from the business plan).
- Never promote on r/sewing or r/femalefashionadvice (they ban it).

## Naming (Oct 29)

- **Collection** = the seasonal house looks (SS27 Collection, AW27...). Never call them drops or releases.
- **Drop** = only the numbered, sewist-designed limited piece (Drop 01, Jun 24, 2027).
- **The collection preview** (Oct 30, replaces "the wardrobe story") = Cat sews and wears pieces
  chosen from the coming collection's looks before launch (SS27 Collection Preview). Nothing new is
  designed and no extra garments. Social only. Don't call it a capsule or wardrobe story in copy.

## Look

- Brand direction: high fashion mixed with street style (Paris, Berlin, London, Scandi), cutting-edge,
  not cliché Paris. Taste references and the yes/never lists are in the **taste brief** (Claude Doc,
  linked from the business plan); read it before any promo, editorial or image work.
- App look: Contour Line on an oxblood near-black ground #120805 (Nov 6; true black before), champagne lines
  (#DFBE84) and ivory text (#F3DFB7), muted #A28C69, shadows a deeper oxblood #080302, never black. Square outline buttons; no shadows or rounded cards. One fill only (Oct 31): champagne
  fill is for the main button; a picked choice is a heavier champagne outline, never a fill. One job per screen, one main
  button, few words.
- Style screen picture windows (Nov 11, Cat's B): chalk #e9e4da inside the window with dark ink lines (#2a2622, #1d1a17)
  and ink-on-white flats, so garments read clearly; everything around the windows stays black.
- Fonts (three, checked in the code Oct 30): **Chakra Petch** for the wordmark, buttons and small
  spaced labels; **Archivo**, capitals at its normal width, for h1/h2 headings; **Space Grotesk** for body text.
  No Bodoni or italic serif (removed Oct 18). Mock-ups, option sheets and video titles use the same
  three. **One width, always** (Cat, Nov 2): never narrow, flatten or stretch a font to fit
  (no font-stretch other than 100%, no scaleX on text); wrap or reword instead.
- Heading sizes (Cat, Nov 3): big Archivo headings are never small and bold, which reads squished.
  The Style title ("Your dress") matches the step name exactly: 40px, weight 600, on phone and computer
  (Nov 4, B Taller Type); "Your trousers" wraps to two lines on a phone rather than shrinking.
- Buttons in Chakra Petch use only the loaded weights, 400 and 500; a heavier weight is a bold the phone
  fakes and reads squished (Nov 4). The Style Next button is 16px, weight 500, spaced 0.1em.
- The three steps in the header stay **Measure, Style, Print**. Every screen says where you've landed and
  holds the sewist's hand (Cat, Nov 3: big capitals for a whole choice are "overwhelming and jarring"). Print
  opens on a modest "Your summary" heading, one line on what the page is for, then the garment's name in
  reading type.
- Logo: R1 wordmark (MAISON / TRACÉ stacked, MT favicon); loading screen L1 slim-dart piece (Oct 21).
- **Brand palette for marketing** (Oct 25): Champagne #c9b48a, Oxblood #5a1f24, Python Aqua (the
  app's one-way python print), Ink #1d2a3a, Chalk #e9e4da, labels on ivory #f3dfb7, oxblood ground #120805 (black before Nov 6).
  These are not the app's fabric colour list.
- Kit email colours: oxblood #120805, champagne #DFBE84, ivory #F3DFB7, taupe #A28C69 (black / #D9C49B / #EFE4CC / #9A8E78 before Nov 6).
- Editorial imagery is real photography. No line-drawn people on editorial pages, and **no AI
  people** (the AI-model promo film is parked; Cat is the face).
- The dress form is the same shape everywhere in the app; menswear keeps its own form.
